Method

Column A, Gameweek 1. Three sources published full XIs for all twenty clubs. Every name is checked against the official Premier League feed first: a source naming a player the feed has ruled out is stale rather than dissenting, and FPL Assistant failed that test seven times (Minteh, out until late November, is the clearest). So the vote count that matters is RotoWire against Yahoo, with OneFPL and ESPN breaking ties on the bigger clubs. 3/3 means unanimous, 1/3 means one source alone.

Column B, the season. Built from the ranking blend — FPL's own draft rank, Draft Fantasy's expected points, the LöfLife sheet and the consensus ADP from fifteen real drafts — with temporary injuries deliberately ignored, then read against each club's summer business. It answers whose shirt it is in October, not who starts on Saturday.

The gap between the two columns is the useful part. A man in A but not B is renting a shirt while someone is injured. A man in B but not A is the one to hold through a quiet fortnight.
